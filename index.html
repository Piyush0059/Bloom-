Checking the configured voice…
Bloom plays no default/system voice. Voice output activates only after a consented voice ID, provider key, and server-side consent confirmation are configured. Google Gemini voice replication requires a clean 10–30 second reference recording from the same adult speaker plus a separate consent recording spoken by the voice owner. The uploaded 8-second clip is too short and cannot be used alone.
This note stays in this browser's local storage. When Memory is enabled, it is sent to the private Bloom backend with a chat request.
Voice promise: Bloom will not play a generic browser/system voice. Spoken replies remain unavailable until a consented voice provider is connected. Microphone dictation, where supported, is input-only and may be processed by your browser's speech-recognition service.
Privacy note: when you send a message, its text and conversation context are sent to Google Gemini to generate a response. Saved chats and settings stay in this browser unless you export or clear them. This is a client-side beta, not a secure multi-user production service.
